## About the Series

**One Piece** is a *Japanese manga* series written and illustrated by **Eiichiro Oda**. It began in *Weekly Shonen Jump* in 1997, and the anime by Toei Animation premiered in 1999. It is one of the best-selling manga series of all time. ~~It is a short and quiet story.~~ It is a long and exciting adventure!

## Story in a Nutshell

Luffy dreams of finding the legendary treasure called ***the One Piece*** and becoming the Pirate King. Along the way he gathers a crew called the **Straw Hat Pirates**.

### Main Characters

| Character | Role | Special Skill |
|-----------|------|---------------|
| Monkey D. Luffy | Captain | Rubber body (`Gomu Gomu no Mi`) |
| Roronoa Zoro | Swordsman | Three-sword fighting style |
| Nami | Navigator | Navigation and weather skills |
| Sanji | Cook | Powerful kicks |
